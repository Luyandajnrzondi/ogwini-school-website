Our History

The Ogwini story

Ogwini Comprehensive Technical High School was established to answer a clear community need: a school that could combine rigorous academic teaching with genuine, workshop-based technical training. Over more than three decades, the school has grown from a handful of classrooms into a full comprehensive campus with dedicated laboratories, technical workshops, and sporting facilities.

Generations of learners have passed through our gates — many going on to universities, TVET colleges, apprenticeships and successful careers in engineering, construction, ICT and commerce. Today, Ogwini remains rooted in the same founding purpose: preparing every learner, academic or technical, for a future built on real skill.

Principal's Desk

A message from the Principal

It is my privilege to lead a school with such a clear sense of purpose. At Ogwini, we do not separate academic ambition from technical skill — we treat them as two halves of the same goal: preparing capable, confident young people for the world beyond matric.

I encourage every parent and learner considering Ogwini to visit our campus, meet our staff, and see our workshops in action. You will find a school community that takes discipline seriously, celebrates achievement, and never stops striving for better.

Mr. S. Ndlovu
Principal
