Technical Education

From drawing board to workshop floor.

Ogwini's technical stream gives learners real tools, real materials and real problems to solve — building the practical skill base for engineering, construction, electrical trades and ICT careers.

Overview

Why technical education matters here

Technical subjects at Ogwini are taught the way they're practised in industry: hands-on, safety-first, and grounded in real specifications. Learners move between theory in the classroom and application in the workshop, building a portfolio of practical work that stands alongside their academic results.

Every technical subject is assessed through both written theory and Practical Assessment Tasks (PATs), giving learners a qualification that speaks directly to employers, colleges and universities.

Subjects Offered

Six technical disciplines

EGD

Engineering Graphics & Design

Technical drawing, orthographic and isometric projection, and CAD fundamentals.

CIV

Civil Technology

Construction, structural systems, surveying and building science.

ELEC

Electrical Technology

Circuits, power systems, control logic and safe electrical practice.

MECH

Mechanical Technology

Machining, fitting, welding and mechanical systems design.

IT

Information Technology

Programming, systems analysis, databases and software development.

CAT

Computer Applications Technology

Practical computer literacy, spreadsheets, databases and web basics.

Technical Workshops

Where theory becomes practice

Our Civil, Electrical and Mechanical Technology workshops are equipped with dedicated tools, safety equipment and demonstration stations, giving every learner supervised, hands-on time with real materials.

Practical projects — from wired circuit boards to small structural builds — form part of every learner's PAT portfolio.
