Admission Requirements

Who can apply

Admission to Grade 8 is open to learners who have successfully completed Grade 7. Placement into a technical subject stream is based on space availability and, where applicable, a short subject orientation session. Learners applying to Grades 9–12 will be assessed based on their most recent academic record and subject availability.

Application Process

Four steps to enrolment

Download Application Form

Submit Required Documents

Verification Process

Our admissions team verifies documents and confirms subject or stream availability.

Admission Outcome
