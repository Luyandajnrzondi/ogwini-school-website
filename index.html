A Word From The Principal

A school built on discipline, skill, and belief in every learner.

Ogwini Comprehensive Technical High School was founded on a simple belief: that every learner deserves both a strong academic foundation and real, practical skill. Our classrooms, laboratories and workshops work together to prepare young people not only for examinations, but for life beyond them.

We are proud of the discipline, respect and innovation our learners carry with them — qualities that will serve them in further study, in trades, and in the workplace. I welcome you to explore our school and to consider Ogwini for your child's journey.

Mr. S. Ndlovu
Principal, Ogwini Comprehensive Technical High School
