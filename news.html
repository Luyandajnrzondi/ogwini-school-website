Announcement 14 July 2026

School Reopens for Term 3

Learners return on Monday, 14 July, for the start of Term 3. Full uniform and stationery are expected from day one. The Term 3 class timetable and updated academic calendar are now posted on the notice board outside the admin block, and copies are available from class teachers.

Grade 12 2 July 2026

Grade 12 Career Expo

Representatives from TVET colleges, universities and local industry partners visited Ogwini to meet with our Grade 12 learners. Sessions covered application processes, bursary opportunities and career pathways in engineering, ICT and commerce — directly building on subjects taught in our technical and academic streams.

Sport 24 June 2026

Sports Tournament Success

Our First XI soccer team and senior netball squad both advanced to the district finals after strong performances at the inter-schools tournament. Congratulations to all players and coaches for their discipline and teamwork throughout the season.

Academics 18 June 2026

Academic Awards Ceremony

Top-performing learners across all grades were recognised at the Term 2 Academic Awards Ceremony, with special recognition for outstanding results in Mathematics, Physical Sciences and the technical subjects.
