Academics

A curriculum built for depth and choice.

From languages to sciences, commerce to humanities — our FET curriculum is structured to give every learner a strong academic pathway alongside their technical studies.

Curriculum Overview

Subjects grouped by department

All learners take a foundation of languages, mathematics and Life Orientation, then choose from Sciences, Commerce or Humanities electives — or combine academic subjects with a technical stream. Departmental teams coordinate closely to track learner progress across all subjects.

Examination Information

Preparing for assessments

Term and common examinations are scheduled well in advance, with study guides issued by each department. Grade 12 learners receive additional preparation through the Matric Support Programme, including after-school study sessions and past-paper practice.

Timetables

Class & exam schedules

Weekly class timetables are distributed at the start of each term and posted on the notice board outside the admin block. Examination timetables are published at least two weeks before assessments begin, and are also available for download.

Learner Support Programmes

Help every learner needs, when they need it

Learner Support Educators

Dedicated staff assist learners with barriers to learning, adapted resources and one-on-one guidance.

Matric Support Programme

After-school sessions, past-paper practice and mentorship for Grade 12 learners.

Study Skills Workshops

Termly sessions on time management, note-taking and exam technique for all grades.

Career & Subject Guidance

Individual guidance sessions to help learners choose subjects aligned with future goals.
